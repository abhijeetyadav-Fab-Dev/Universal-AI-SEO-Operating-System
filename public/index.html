                Traditional SEO ranks blue links on Google. GEO/AEO optimizes your entities so ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Gemini cite your brand as the direct source of truth.
              

              
    
                
  • Entity Disambiguation: Anchor Organization & Author in Wikidata & Crunchbase.
    • 
                
  • Direct Q&A Hierarchy: Format H2s as exact question strings with immediate 40-word concise answers.
    • 
                
  • JSON-LD Grounding: Implement FAQPage & Event schemas so scrapers don't hallucinate dates.
